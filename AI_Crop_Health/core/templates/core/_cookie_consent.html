{% comment %} Cookie consent banner. Design decisions worth knowing: * Strictly necessary cookies (session, CSRF) are NOT consented to - they are required for login and form security, and the law does not require consent for them. The banner says so rather than pretending otherwise. * Analytics is the only optional category, and the script is injected ONLY after the visitor accepts. Loading a tracker first and asking afterwards is the common mistake that makes a banner legally useless. * "Reject" is as prominent as "Accept". A banner where refusing is harder than agreeing is not valid consent. * The choice is stored in localStorage, not a cookie, so declining does not itself set a cookie. {% endcomment %}
