  • Email delivery provider — delivers login codes and notifications to your email address. We do not send login codes by SMS.

6. How we protect your data

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Login uses one-time codes, so there is no password for us to lose.
  • Each sensor device has its own key, so one compromised device cannot affect another farmer's field.
  • Identity documents are not reachable by search engines.
  • Access to verification documents is limited to staff who review them.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

7. Cookies

Anything optional, such as analytics, is only set after you agree to it in the cookie banner, and you can change your mind at any time.

8. Children

9. Automated decisions, and their limits

Disease diagnosis is produced by a machine-learning model. It is advisory and can be wrong. Its accuracy has not been independently measured. Always confirm with a qualified agronomist or your local agricultural extension officer before spending money on treatment or destroying a crop. No automated decision here legally binds you.
